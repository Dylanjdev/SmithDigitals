Landscaping Lead Generation Website

Built to turn quote requests into booked jobs.

McGuire's Lawn & Landscaping needed a site that did one thing really well: capture local demand and convert that traffic into real customers through a simple, high-intent quote flow.

Conversion

6 became customers

Focus

Local lead generation

The challenge

Get more qualified local leads.

Before launch, the business needed stronger online visibility and a cleaner path for visitors to request services. The goal was to increase booked work by simplifying first contact.

What I built

A conversion-first quote funnel.

Built a responsive website optimized for local landscaping searches.

Designed a streamlined free quote form to reduce friction and improve completion rates.

Structured service pages and messaging around high-intent homeowner needs.

Improved technical and on-page SEO to increase visibility in local results.

Result

Immediate revenue impact after launch.

In one week, the free quote form generated 6 submissions and every one of those inquiries turned into a customer. The website is now consistently functioning as a real lead-generation asset, not just an online brochure.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

McGuire's Lawn & Landscaping needed a local lead-generation experience that could turn search intent into a real conversation. The important measure was not traffic alone; it was whether qualified visitors completed a quote request and became customers.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Put the service area to work

Location and service language give nearby homeowners a faster way to determine whether McGuire's is a relevant provider.

02

Make the quote path obvious

The form is treated as a primary conversion path, with supporting content that answers enough questions to make submitting it feel reasonable.

03

Use proof before asking

Service descriptions and page hierarchy establish credibility before the visitor reaches the request step.

04

Optimize for qualified action

The experience is judged by completed quote requests and customer outcomes, not by inflated pageview numbers.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The build combined local SEO, service positioning, and conversion design. Each page decision was evaluated against the question a homeowner is silently asking: can this team solve my problem, in my area, and how do I get an estimate?

For a local service company, a small number of qualified conversions can matter more than a large audience. The site was built around the economics of the lead, not the vanity of the visit.
