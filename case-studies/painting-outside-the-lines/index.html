About the studio

Art for everyone in Lee County.

Painting Outside The Lines Studio opened on Main Street in Pennington Gap to bring accessible, high-quality art experiences to Southwest Virginia. Their philosophy is simple: you don't need experience to create something beautiful. Expert instructors guide every session, walking guests through techniques step by step.

The studio is thoughtfully designed — natural light, comfortable seating, and an atmosphere that makes artistry feel effortless. Whether it's a first date, a birthday party, or a corporate team-building event, they tailor every experience to the group.

Smith Digitals designed and built their website to reflect the warmth and creativity of the studio itself — organized around their class schedule, event offerings, and the story of a space that's already become a community landmark in Lee County, Virginia.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

Painting Outside The Lines Studios needed a digital presence that felt as creative as the experience it offers. Classes, workshops, and artistic services all need visual appeal, but visitors also need practical answers before they book or attend.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Let the work carry the first impression

The visual system gives the studio's creative identity room to lead without turning the page into an image gallery that hides essential information.

02

Separate experiences clearly

Classes, workshops, and services are presented as distinct paths so visitors can move from inspiration to the option that fits them.

03

Write for the unsure visitor

The content helps someone understand what participation feels like, what the studio offers, and what a next step looks like.

04

Protect the conversion path

Calls to action remain easy to find after the expressive sections so a visitor does not have to search for booking or contact information.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The work balanced atmosphere and utility. Visual choices established the studio's personality, while page structure answered the practical questions that determine whether a curious visitor becomes an attendee.

Creative businesses do not have to choose between a distinctive site and a usable one. The strongest experience gives the brand space to surprise people, then gives them a clear route to participate.
