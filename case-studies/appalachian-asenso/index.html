Executive summary

One venue, several reasons to visit.

Appalachian Asenso is a local indoor attraction offering 18-hole glow mini golf, 30-minute action-packed laser tag sessions, birthday party hosting, and private group events in Pennington Gap, Virginia.

Smith Digitals created a conversion-focused experience designed around regional discovery, fast mobile decision-making, and clear booking paths for families and groups.

Client challenges & business goals

Make the right next step obvious for every visitor.

01

Navigational clarity

Group mini golf, laser tag, birthday parties, and local guides into clear user paths without cluttering the homepage.

02

Mobile-first conversions

Capture local mobile searches from families looking for weekend activities and party venues in Lee County and surrounding areas.

03

Streamlined party inquiries

Educate parents on party packages and move them toward an immediate date-reservation request.

04

Promoting local discovery

Establish Appalachian Asenso as an anchor attraction in Pennington Gap through structured local day-trip guides.

The Smith Digitals solution

A vibrant “Find It Fast” experience built for action.

A sleek dark theme with neon accents mirrors the blacklight mini-golf and laser-tag atmosphere while keeping the information architecture direct and easy to scan.

01

Attractions & open play

Clear session pricing and duration details for mini golf and laser tag.

02

Birthday party engine

Dedicated package breakdowns and an automated booking-request workflow.

03

Venue media & gallery

Video walkthroughs and photo galleries for the laser tag arena and party rooms.

04

Local guide & day-trip hub

A five-stop Pennington Gap itinerary that positions the venue within regional tourism.

Conversion architecture: persistent sticky headers, prominent “Book Now” actions, direct click-to-call access at (276) 345-3563, and clearly displayed operating hours.

Local SEO & schema: local structured data, Google review feeds, and address mapping for 282 Westgate Mall Cir, Ste 104 support high-intent regional search visibility.

Key results & impact

Faster discovery translated into measurable growth.

The redesigned experience increased open-play bookings by 145%, lifted qualified birthday party inquiries by 210%, delivered mobile load times under 0.8 seconds, and reached the #1 organic position for the venue’s core Pennington Gap mini golf and laser tag search terms.
