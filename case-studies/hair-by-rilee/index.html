Local SEO + Service Positioning

Hair By Rilee: a digital system built around the work.

Hair By Rilee needed a focused landing page that could introduce the stylist, communicate the service, and make the business easier to discover in Lee County, Virginia.

Visit Hair By Rilee website →

Market

Lee County, VA

Focus

Local search visibility

Stack

Responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript

The challenge

Turn a social-first local service into a searchable, credible web presence with enough clarity for new visitors to understand the offer and get in touch.

What I built

An SEO-optimized landing page with local business schema markup, geo-targeted content, responsive presentation, and social media integration.

Built a responsive landing page that introduces the stylist and services clearly.

Added local business structured data and location-focused page signals.

Connected the site experience to the brand's social media presence.

Kept the path from discovery to contact simple for prospective clients.

Result

Hair By Rilee now has a concise digital foundation that supports local discovery and gives prospective clients a clear first impression.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

Hair By Rilee needed a focused online presence for a local service business whose discovery had largely depended on referrals and social channels. The landing page had to explain the service, establish local relevance, and give a prospective client a clear way forward.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Build around local intent

Location signals and service language help search engines connect the page with people looking for a stylist in Lee County, Virginia.

02

Keep the page focused

A single purposeful landing page avoids making a small service business feel larger or more complicated than it is.

03

Connect web and social

Social media supports ongoing relationship-building, while the website provides a stable, searchable reference point.

04

Make contact uncomplicated

The page moves visitors from first impression to inquiry without burying the next step beneath unnecessary content.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The build combined responsive front-end work with local SEO fundamentals and business schema markup. The goal was a compact experience that could do several jobs well: introduce the stylist, explain the offer, and support local discovery.

Small businesses do not need a sprawling site to look credible. They need the right information, in the right order, connected to the way customers actually search and decide.
