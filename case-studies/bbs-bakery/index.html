Bakery Website + Local SEO

From local bakery brand to online orders in motion.

BBS Bakery needed more than a pretty site. They needed a conversion-focused online presence that made it easy for customers to browse, order, and check out from any device.

SEO timeline

Ranking outcome

Above Blackbird Bakery

The challenge

Turn bakery traffic into online buyers.

BBS Bakery had a strong local product and loyal customers, but needed a modern site that could support ordering at scale and improve visibility in local search. The business goal was clear: reduce purchase friction and grow discoverability in nearby markets.

What I built

Square-powered checkout plus local SEO fundamentals.

Integrated online ordering through the Square Checkout API.

Designed a responsive shopping experience for mobile-first users.

Refined page structure and copy for local-intent search queries.

Improved technical SEO signals for indexing and ranking momentum.

Result

Ranking gains in less than one quarter.

Within just 3 months of launch, BBS Bakery began ranking above Blackbird Bakery in Bristol, VA for relevant local search visibility. Combined with streamlined Square checkout, that meant both stronger discovery and better conversion once visitors landed on the site.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

BBS Bakery's opportunity was both local and practical: make the business easier to find, then make the path from appetite to completed order feel obvious. The site needed to support a real purchase action instead of stopping at a phone number or a static menu.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Lead with the product

The experience keeps the bakery offer and ordering path close to the surface so visitors do not need to decode the business before acting.

02

Reduce checkout friction

Square Checkout provided a familiar transaction flow while the website handled discovery, product context, and the handoff into purchase.

03

Build local relevance

Page structure, copy, and metadata were shaped around the way nearby customers search for bakery products and services.

04

Design for the first visit

Responsive layouts and clear hierarchy help a customer browse from a phone, often while deciding what to order in the moment.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The work connected three layers that are often treated separately: brand presentation, ecommerce behavior, and local search. That meant the order flow could not be designed in isolation from the page content, and SEO could not be treated as a final metadata pass.

For a local food business, visibility only matters when the next action is simple. The strongest improvement came from treating search discovery and ordering as one customer journey rather than two separate projects.
