01 · Executive summary

Technical precision translated into an enterprise buying experience.

Power Pasture™ delivers local, site-specific renewable energy infrastructure by combining geothermal, hydro, solar, and wind into integrated generation models. Its systems are designed for power-intensive applications including high-density data centers, advanced manufacturing, hospital campuses, and master-planned residential communities.

Smith Digitals architected and deployed the Power Pasture™ enterprise platform—an interactive dark-mode experience with technical resource schematics and a B2B lead funnel tailored to infrastructure decision-makers.
