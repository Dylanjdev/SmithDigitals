Ecommerce + Inventory Operations

M&M Wholesale Store: a digital system built around the work.

M&M Wholesale Store needed more than a catalog. The platform had to support a changing inventory, secure access, and a smoother wholesale workflow for the people running the business.

Visit Website →

Platform

Full-stack ecommerce

Operations

Custom admin dashboard

Infrastructure

Firebase-backed data

The challenge

Create a wholesale storefront that could keep product information current without making every inventory update a manual website edit.

What I built

A connected ecommerce system with real-time product updates, secure authentication, and a custom admin experience for managing the catalog behind the storefront.

Built a responsive product browsing and purchasing experience for wholesale customers.

Created an admin dashboard for managing inventory and product information.

Connected the platform to Firebase for real-time data and secure authentication.

Structured the experience so the public storefront and internal operations work as one system.

Result

M&M Wholesale Store now has a stronger foundation for selling online and managing the operational work that keeps the catalog accurate.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

M&M Wholesale Store needed a digital platform that could serve customers and the people operating the catalog behind the scenes. The project therefore had two audiences: shoppers who need a dependable storefront and administrators who need control over changing inventory.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Treat inventory as live data

Product information belongs in a system that can be updated as the business changes, rather than being trapped in static page markup.

02

Separate public and admin needs

The storefront stays focused on browsing and purchasing while the dashboard provides the tools needed for internal management.

03

Use authentication deliberately

Secure access protects operational controls without adding unnecessary friction to the public shopping experience.

04

Design for continued growth

A full-stack foundation leaves room for future product, order, and workflow improvements instead of closing the door at launch.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The work connected interface design with data and access decisions. The result is not only a customer-facing ecommerce page, but an operational base that can support the business as the catalog and wholesale process evolve.

Ecommerce success depends on the system behind the storefront. When inventory and administration are considered early, the customer experience can stay accurate without creating a second full-time job for the owner.
