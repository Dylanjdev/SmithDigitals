Meet your instructor

Sam Davis, Founder & Instructor

Sam Davis is the founder and lead instructor at Fit & Fierce. She started the studio with a clear mission: give women in Lee County a fitness space that genuinely welcomes them — regardless of size, experience, or ability.

Behind the build

The decisions were shaped by the business, not by a template.

Fit & Fierce had a clear point of view and a strong community, but that feeling had to translate online for someone who had never attended a class. The experience needed to make beginners feel invited while preserving the energy and confidence of the studio.

Design and strategy decisions

What made this project specific to the client.

01

Make belonging visible

The copy and visual hierarchy lead with inclusivity, body positivity, and zero judgment so the right audience can recognize itself quickly.

02

Use social as a living schedule

Facebook remains the active source for class updates, while the website provides the stable brand and location context around it.

03

Balance energy with clarity

Bold language and movement-oriented presentation are paired with straightforward details about classes, location, and who can participate.

04

Keep the invitation low-pressure

The calls to action let a visitor explore the studio or check current updates without forcing a commitment before trust exists.

How the work came together

Strategy, experience, and implementation in one loop.

The project was shaped around audience confidence first. Instead of presenting fitness as a test people must pass, the page explains the atmosphere, the instructor, and the community so a first-time visitor can imagine showing up.

A service brand can be differentiated by the emotional experience it promises, not only by its list of offerings. Fit & Fierce's strongest asset was its sense of welcome, so the website made that asset legible before the first class.
