01 · Executive summary

A scalable digital home for the whole county.

Lee County Tourism, operating as I Love Lee, serves as the official destination marketing organization for Lee County, Virginia—an Appalachian hub for outdoor adventure, historical landmarks, and locally owned businesses.

Smith Digitals built the scalable infrastructure behind the official DiscoverLeeVa destination portal, designed to aggregate regional attractions, centralize community events, and support local commerce through dedicated search-optimized business pages.

02 · Core strategic pillars

Shared infrastructure that helps the region compound attention.

01

Shared community calendar

Centralized event hub

Local organizations, artisans, and venue hosts can aggregate upcoming county events in one accessible destination.

Increased visitor retention

Real-time updates on performances, festivals, and community gatherings keep tourists and residents engaged.

02

Free dedicated business directory pages

Individual SEO landing pages

Every local business receives a dedicated, search-optimized page designed to maximize organic indexation.

Hyper-local backlinks

Contextual directory links strengthen domain authority and search visibility for dining, lodging, retail, and service partners.

A three-month organic performance breakthrough.
